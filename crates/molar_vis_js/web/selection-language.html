Select atoms within one molecule. Distances are in nm. Click an example to copy.

Common selections

  • Every atom
  • Protein atoms
  • Backbone atoms
  • Water molecules
  • Hydrogen atoms

Also: sidechain, now (not water), noh (no H), polh and apolh (polar / apolar H).

Combine selections

  • Protein without H
  • Either chain
  • Exclude water
  • Group conditions

Use and, or, not. Group mixed and/or with parentheses. Keywords and names are case-sensitive.

Names, residues and indices

  • Atom names
  • Starts with C
  • Residue names
  • Chain IDs
  • File residue IDs
  • First ten residues
  • Atom indices

Space-separated values match any value. Ranges are inclusive. /…/ matches full names.

Numbers and coordinates

  • Coordinate slab (nm)
  • Mass filter
  • Charged atoms
  • B-factor filter
  • Math in comparisons

Fields: x, y, z, mass, charge, vdw, occupancy (occ), bfactor (beta), vx/vy/vz, fx/fy/fz. Math: + − * / ^; abs, sqrt, sin, cos.

Neighbors and whole groups

  • Atoms near a ligand
  • Include the ligand
  • Periodic neighbors
  • Whole matching residues
  • Whole matching chains

within excludes its target; self includes it. Distances are in nm. pbc uses the periodic box; pbc 110 enables x and y only.

Points, lines and planes

  • Near a fixed point
  • Distance from a point
  • Distance from the z axis
  • Distance from a plane

Points can also be com of (protein) or pos 0 of (protein): the center of mass or first selected atom. Comparisons: < <= > >= == !=.

index and resindex start at 0. resid uses IDs from the structure file.
